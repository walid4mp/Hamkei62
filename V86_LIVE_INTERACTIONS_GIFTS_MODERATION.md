# SocialNova V86 — Live Interactions, Moderation & Gift FX

## Live comments
- Persistent Live comments via `LiveComment`.
- Tap a comment/avatar to open the author's profile.
- Tap reply to set a threaded reply target.
- Long-press a comment for reply / pin / delete actions.
- Host and assigned Live moderators can pin/delete comments.

## Live moderators
- Host can choose people from the host's followers.
- Two roles: `MODERATOR` and `ASSISTANT`.
- Moderator permissions cover comment deletion, pinning and chat moderation; the moderator role can also manage moderators.

## Gifts
- Catalog expanded to 237+ gifts.
- Gift records now carry `effectKey`, `effectMs`, and `soundKey`.
- Dedicated visual treatments for crown, lion, rocket, sports car and airplane gifts.
- Live gift score is displayed and can contribute to an active challenge score.

## Live tapping
- Whole video stage accepts taps/double taps for Live tap count.
- Tap count remains visible in the Live HUD.
