# SocialNova V7 — Stories + Repost + Test Accounts

## Stories
- Instagram-style ❤️ reaction on stories.
- Story replies are saved as StoryReply.
- Story replies/reactions are also inserted into the author's direct Messenger conversation.
- Notifications are created for story reactions/replies.

## Repost
- Repost toggle returns the current repost state and exact repost count.
- Feed now includes public posts reposted by the current user or followed users, so reposts remain visible after refresh.
