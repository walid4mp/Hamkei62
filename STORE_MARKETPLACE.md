# SocialNova Store / Marketplace

Added a dedicated marketplace tab in the bottom navigation.

## Included
- Store page separate from the timeline.
- Categories: clothing, electronics, gifts, sports.
- Search and filters by price and geographic listing location.
- Sort by latest, views, and purchase requests.
- User-created sale listings with product image, price, description, location and optional coupon/discount.
- Product view counter and purchase-request counter.
- Seller rating/review system restricted to users who have placed an order request.
- Discount percentage and coupon code fields.
- Product detail page with quantity, order request, seller rating summary and review action.

## Important
The current implementation records a store order request; it does not process real-world card/payment settlement. A payment provider can be integrated later with its own age/identity and merchant requirements.
